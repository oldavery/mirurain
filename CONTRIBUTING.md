# Contributing

First off, thanks for being interested in contributing to Mirurain, with your support we'll become even better! Before you get started there's some guidelines and information you'll need to know before helping us.

## AI

The usage of AI against Mirurain's codebase is disallowed unless it falls under an exemption detailed in our `AGENTS.md`.

## Commit Messages

Mirurain uses [Conventional Commits](https://www.conventionalcommits.org/en/v1.0.0/) to help make changes quickly profilable, we ask that you at minimum follow their commit message syntax.

## Developer Certificate of Origin

By contributing to Mirurain you understand and accept our [Developer Certificate of Origin](https://forge.kemonomimi.moe/avery/mirurain/src/branch/main/DCO) and agree to sign off all commits.

## Signing Commits

We request that all commits be signed, you can learn how to sign commits [here](https://git-scm.com/book/en/v2/Git-Tools-Signing-Your-Work), this helps us ensure changes are authored by you.
